About

Creative thinking. Hands-on design.

I've spent more than 20 years designing brands, building digital experiences and helping teams turn complicated ideas into something clear, useful and memorable.

My background spans creative direction, brand identity, illustration, UX and lifecycle marketing. At Ancestry, I worked on campaigns and customer experiences that combined creative thinking with measurable business results. Before that, I led brand and design work at Jive, Elevate Marketing and Logoworks.

I enjoy the strategy as much as the design itself. I'm also exploring how AI can improve creative workflows, open up new possibilities and give designers more time to do their best work.

Outside of design, I play piano for musical improv shows. It's a different kind of creative problem-solving, and a good reminder that some of the best ideas come from listening and collaborating.
