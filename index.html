Featured work / Ancestry

DNA Onboarding Redesign

Collaborating on creative direction and translating strategy into effective lifecycle email design.

+139.36% click-rate lift*

Select a design to explore. Scroll inside the tablet at any time.

The challenge & approach

Make the wait for DNA results more valuable.

Our hypothesis: educating customers before their DNA results arrived would help them understand Ancestry's features, engage more deeply, and support long-term cross-selling. The team tested a redesigned onboarding email experience explaining what to expect from results and how to use key features.

Results & impact
+139.36%Click-rate lift
+28.57%Family history signup-rate lift
+16.48%Family History Gross Subscriber Additions rate lift
+17.58%Overall Gross Subscriber Additions rate lift

30% lower email unsubscribe rate was also reported in the test.

Reported team test results; not individually attributed. GSA stands for Gross Subscriber Additions. These outcomes measure engagement and conversion signals, not proven long-term cross-selling.
