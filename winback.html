Featured work / Ancestry / February 2024

Winback Campaign

Same audience. Different offer. Dramatically different results.

+898% value acquired*

How do you reconnect with customers whose subscriptions have lapsed? For Ancestry's February Flash Sale, our team tested a 40%-off Family History subscription offer against a $69 DNA offer for lapsed customers without DNA. I designed both the original and test email creative, bringing each offer and messaging approach to life.

Original / DNA offer

Control email, $69 DNA offer

Test / Family History offer

40% off a semiannual subscription

Results & impact

The Family History offer generated substantially more value and subscriber conversions, despite essentially unchanged email click rates. The test showed how offer relevance can matter more than click volume.

+898%Revenue / value acquired
+218%Gross Subscriber Additions conversion rate
0.17%Click rate, approximately unchanged

*Reported February Flash Sale test results. Performance reflects offer, audience and messaging strategy as well as creative execution; not attributable to visual design alone.
